# User Stories — General Public

- As an interested reader, I want a simple landing page that explains what the *Res Gestae* is and how to explore it.
- As an interested reader, I want an easy reading mode with brief annotations so I can understand who/what/where without academic jargon.
- As an interested reader, I want an overview map/timeline to grasp the places and periods mentioned.
- As an interested reader, I want a quick way to share a passage (permalink) with friends or social channels.
- As an interested reader, I want clear licensing and credit info so I know how I may reuse the material.
